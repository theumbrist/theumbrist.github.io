Privacy Policy — Can You Get This?

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This application is used only by the owner of the "Can You Get This?" channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API it uploads and schedules videos on our own channel, posts our own comments, and reads public comments on our own videos to find the first correct answer to each puzzle.

What it stores

It keeps only the public display name of the first correct guesser, to credit them in the answer comment. No other personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone.

Google user data

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

This application uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).
